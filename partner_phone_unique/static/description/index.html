Good to know

What about the duplicates I already have?

They stay and the installation succeeds. The check applies as soon as a number is changed or a new contact is added.

Can a company and its employee share a phone?

No: one number belongs to one contact, including contacts of the same company. Give the shared line to the company only.

How do I clean up the duplicates I already have?

Use the standard Merge action of Contacts: it works with this module and the merged contact keeps the number.

Does Duplicate still work on a contact?

Yes. The copy simply starts without the phone number.

Are contacts without a phone affected?

No. An empty phone, text without digits, or a placeholder under 5 digits like 0 or 000 is never a duplicate.

Is a local number the same as its international form?

Only if the digits are the same: +1 650 555 0123 and 001 650 555 0123 match, 650 555 0123 is a different number.
